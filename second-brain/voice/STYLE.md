# Voice

Read this before writing anything in `drafts/`. It is deliberately incomplete —
it gets sharp only as samples accumulate in `voice/samples/`. Until then, the
honest default is: **write plainly and flag that the voice is unverified.**

## How to build voice from the vault

Priority order when drafting. Higher sources of voice override lower ones.

1. **`voice/samples/`** — things the owner actually published. Match sentence
   length, paragraph rhythm, how arguments open and close, what gets hedged and
   what gets asserted flatly.
2. **`## My take` sections** in notes, and `daily/` entries. This is unedited
   thinking: the opinions are real and the phrasing is native.
3. **`notes/` with `origin: mine` or `mixed`.**
4. **`notes/` with `origin: ai-distilled`** — use for facts and structure only.
   Never lift its prose into a draft. It was written to be retrieved, not read.

If a draft can only be built from level 4, say so in the handoff: it is a
research memo with citations, and the owner still has to write the piece.

## Rules that hold regardless of samples

- **One claim per sentence.** Long sentences that stack three ideas are where
  vagueness hides.
- **Concrete before abstract.** The example comes first, the principle second.
- **No throat-clearing.** Cut "In today's fast-paced world", "It's important to
  note that", "Let's dive in". Start at the first real sentence.
- **Assert or attribute.** Either state it, or say who said it. Avoid
  "many experts believe" and "it is widely considered".
- **Kill the rule of three when it is decoration.** Three parallel items because
  there are three real items, not because the rhythm sounds finished.
- **No inflated stakes.** Not "revolutionary", not "game-changing", not "a
  testament to". If the thing is good, show what it does.
- **Own the uncertainty.** "I think", "I have not tested this at scale", "this
  may not hold for X" are stronger than false confidence, and they age well.

## Samples

Drop published pieces into `voice/samples/` as markdown. Filename:
`YYYY-MM-DD - Title.md`. Frontmatter should carry `format:` and `platform:` so
drafting can match the right register. Three to five samples per format is
enough to be useful.
