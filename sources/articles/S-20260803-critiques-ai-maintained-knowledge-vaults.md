# Critiques of AI-maintained knowledge vaults

## Why captured

The counter-case. What these systems get wrong.

## Source text

Synthesis of the critical position across three sources. (1) AI slop: over time the owner cannot tell whether content was written by them or by an AI, and their own thoughts get diminished. AI summaries replace the thinking that makes knowledge stick. (2) Loss of ownership of connections: the power lies in the deliberately created graph of notes; relations created by AI 'will not count for anything since they are not made by you'. (3) The orphan problem: vault decay is a graph problem, not a note-quality problem. The value of a note comes from its connections; an orphan with zero backlinks may have great content but is functionally invisible. (4) Confident mediocrity: without human review the agent produces output that gets shipped and accepted; iterations without review pull the vault toward average internet quality and eventually it is useless. Counter-position from Dubois: do not keep AI out of the vault, put it in charge of the plumbing - the mechanical work of filing, linking, and maintenance, not the thinking.
